## Scheme: PM-KISAN Samman Nidhi

- **Category:** Agriculture

- **Source URL:** https://pmkisan.gov.in/

- **Description:** Income support of Rs. 6,000 per year in three equal installments to all landholding farmer families.

- **Eligibility:** All landholding farmers with cultivable land ownership.

- **Benefits:** Rs. 6,000 per year paid directly to bank accounts.

- **Required Documents:** Aadhaar card, land ownership papers, bank passbook, mobile number.

- **How to Apply:** Register online via PM-KISAN portal or through local Common Service Centers (CSC).

- **Deadlines:** Open year-round for new enrollments.

- **After Applying:** Check beneficiary status using Aadhaar or mobile number on the official portal. Contact district agriculture officer if delayed.

## Scheme: Telangana Rythu Bharosa

- **Category:** Agriculture

- **Source URL:** https://rythubharosa.telangana.gov.in/

- **Description:** Investment support scheme for farmers to cover agricultural input costs.

- **Eligibility:** Resident farmers of Telangana owning cultivable agricultural land.

- **Benefits:** Financial assistance per acre per crop season.

- **Required Documents:** Pattedar Passbook, Aadhaar card, bank account details.

- **How to Apply:** Applications processed through state agriculture department records and Rythu Vedikas.

- **Deadlines:** Announced seasonally before crop sowing periods.

- **After Applying:** Verify fund credit in linked bank account during disbursement windows.


## Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Mudra Yojana

- **Category:** Employment

## Scheme: Pradhan Mantri Ujjwala Yojana

- **Category:** Energy

- **Source URL:** https://pmuy.gov.in/

- **Description:** Free LPG connections to women from BPL families for clean cooking fuel.

- **Eligibility:** Women from BPL families, PMAY, AAY beneficiaries.

- **Benefits:** Free LPG connection with deposit-free cylinder and stove.

- **Required Documents:** BPL ration card, Aadhaar, bank account, age proof.

- **Deadlines:** Open year-round.

- **After Applying:** Wait for LPG connection delivery; track via distributor.


## Scheme: Ayushman Bharat - Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana

- **Category:** Health

- **Source URL:** https://www.pmjay.gov.in/

- **Description:** Health cover of Rs. 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalization.

- **Eligibility:** Poor and vulnerable families as per SECC 2011 database.

- **Benefits:** Cashless treatment up to Rs. 5 lakh per family per year.

- **Required Documents:** Ration card, Aadhaar, age proof, caste certificate if applicable.

- **How to Apply:** Check eligibility on mera.pmjay.gov.in or visit nearest empanelled hospital.

- **Deadlines:** Enrollment is continuous.

- **After Applying:** Get Ayushman card; use at empanelled hospitals for cashless care.


## Scheme: PM Awas Yojana - Gramin

- **Category:** Housing

- **Source URL:** https://pmayg.nic.in/

- **Description:** Housing for all in rural areas - Financial assistance to construct pucca houses with basic amenities.

- **Eligibility:** Homeless families and families living in kutcha houses in rural areas.

- **Benefits:** Rs. 1.20 lakh in plain areas, Rs. 1.30 lakh in hilly areas for house construction.

- **Required Documents:** Aadhaar card, BPL certificate, land ownership document, bank account.

- **How to Apply:** Apply through Gram Panchayat or online via PMAY-G portal.

- **Deadlines:** Varies by state; check local block office.

- **After Applying:** Track application status on PMAY-G portal using registration ID.


## Scheme: National Pension Scheme

- **Category:** Pension

- **Source URL:** https://www.npscra.nsdl.co.in/

- **Description:** NPS - Pension scheme for all citizens to provide retirement income and old age security.

- **Eligibility:** All Indian citizens aged 18-70 years.

- **Benefits:** Tax benefits + pension after 60 years + flexible investment options.

- **Required Documents:** Aadhaar, PAN, bank account, photograph.

- **How to Apply:** Register through Point of Presence (PoP) or eNPS portal.

- **Deadlines:** No deadline; open throughout the year.

- **After Applying:** Receive PRAN card; make regular contributions.


## Scheme: Telangana Aasara Pensions

- **Category:** Pension

- **Source URL:** https://epass.telangana.gov.in/

- **Description:** Monthly pension for elderly, widows, weavers, toddy tappers, and other vulnerable groups.

- **Eligibility:** Telangana residents meeting age/income criteria for each category.

- **Benefits:** Monthly pension credited directly to bank account.

- **Required Documents:** Aadhaar, age certificate, income certificate, bank passbook.

- **How to Apply:** Apply through MeeSeva centers or online via TS ePass portal.

- **Deadlines:** Applications accepted year-round.

- **After Applying:** Check pension status via TS ePass portal.
